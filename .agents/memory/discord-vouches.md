description: The user-approved setup and privacy behavior for customer vouches in this Discord bot.

**Rule:** Configure the destination with an in-Discord administrator command instead of adding a web dashboard. The customer chooses a 1–5 star rating, copies the exact vouch text into the ticket, and the bot posts it with their Discord name/avatar before deleting the ticket. No rating means no deletion. Explain this in French and English.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved ticket deletion after a rated vouch and was told that deletion removes the ticket history.

**How to apply:** Warn the customer that their identity will be public and the ticket will be deleted after submission; preserve the in-Discord setup unless the user asks for a dashboard.
